Trees Hate You (demo)

You just had a picnic in the woods and now it's time to go home. It's just a short hike, and there's nothing in your path except a bunch of trees...should be fine, right? 

Works with a controller (left stick) or a keyboard (WASD or arrow keys).

Update: I recently revisited this project and plan to expand it into a bigger game. So this became the demo of a larger, more horrible adventure.

You can wishlist the full game on Steam (or play the demo there): https://store.steampowered.com/app/4171850/Trees_Hate_You

Please share any feedback either here or on my Discord server.

(Another note: Some people have been reporting low framerates on the web version. If that happens, it may work much better with a different web browser or through the Itch.io app. Or you can try the downloadable version.)
